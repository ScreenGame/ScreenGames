FAQ

Is it free?

Mostly, yes. Racing Master, CarX Street, Wuthering Waves, Genshin Impact, Gangstar Mirage City and Asphalt Legends Unite are free to download and play on Google Play (they offer optional in-app purchases). GTA San Andreas ($6.99) and GTA Vice City ($4.99) are paid apps — their cards show the price up front. GTA V is not a mobile game at all; it's sold for PC and consoles.

Which devices are supported?

The mobile games here run on Android phones and tablets — tap any “Get It Now” button and the store will tell you if your device is compatible before you install. GTA V is the exception: it needs a PC, PlayStation or Xbox, since Rockstar has never released an Android version.

How big are the files?

It varies by game and changes with updates, so each game's Google Play page shows the exact current size before you install. As a rough guide, the classic GTA ports need a couple of GB, while the big open-world RPGs like Genshin Impact and Wuthering Waves need much more once all their resources are downloaded — free up space and use Wi-Fi for those.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed on Screen Games — this page is just a library of links. To install a game you'll use your normal Google account in the Play Store, and some games ask you to sign in with the publisher's account once you're inside the game.
